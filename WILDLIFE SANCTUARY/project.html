Top 10 Wildlife Sanctuaries

Types of Wildlife Sanctuaries

  • Terrestrial Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Marine Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Bird Sanctuary
  • Wetland Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Rescue and Rehabilitation Sanctuary

Jim Corbett National Park, India

Established in 1936, it’s India’s first national park, renowned for its Bengal tiger population and diverse ecosystems. Located in Uttarakhand, it offers rich biodiversity and is a pioneer in wildlife conservation

Serengeti National Park, Tanzania

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, it spans 14,763 km² and is famous for the Great Migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles, along with a large lion population.

Yellowstone National Park, USA

Established in 1872, it’s the world’s first national park, known for its geothermal features like Old Faithful and diverse wildlife including bison and wolves.

Kruger National Park, South Africa

Covering 19,485 km², it’s one of Africa’s largest game reserves, home to the Big Five and part of the Great Limpopo Transfrontier Park.

Galápagos National Park, Ecuador

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, this archipelago is famed for its unique species and role in Charles Darwin’s theory of evolution.

Kanha Tiger Reserve, India

Located in Madhya Pradesh, it inspired Rudyard Kipling’s “The Jungle Book” and is known for its significant tiger population

Chitwan National Park, Nepal

Established in 1973, it’s Nepal’s first national park, offering rich biodiversity, including one-horned rhinoceroses and Bengal tigers.

Banff National Park, Canada

Nestled in the Canadian Rockies, Banff is renowned for its stunning alpine scenery, hot springs, and diverse wildlife.

Yala National Park, Sri Lanka

Home to the world's highest density of leopards, Yala also hosts elephants, crocodiles, and over 200 bird species.

Ranthambore National Park, India

Famous for its large tiger population and historic fort, it offers a unique blend of wildlife and heritage.
